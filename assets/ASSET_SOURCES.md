# Asset sources and provenance

## Weapons and footsteps
- Source: https://github.com/petroulacl/fps-asset-kit
- Flat Guns West/East and Fantozzi footsteps are documented by that source as CC0/public-domain material.
- A copy of the source README is retained at `licenses/FPS_ASSET_KIT_SOURCE_README.md`.

## Human characters
- Source: https://github.com/euuuuuuan/fatal-funnel-public
- Its `CREDITS.md` documents `swat.glb`, `casual-character.glb`, and `worker.glb` (Quaternius Ultimate Modular Men derivatives) as CC0 1.0 with redistribution allowed.
- The source credits file is retained at `licenses/FATAL_FUNNEL_CREDITS.md`.

## Additional enemies
- Source: https://github.com/Ariescar/gobkit-free-assets
- Eight `minion-*.glb` models are supplied under CC0 1.0 and are rigged/animated.
- License copy: `licenses/GOBKIT_CC0.txt`.

## Project-authored assets
- Weapon firing, reload/mechanical, enemy and hit-confirm OGG sounds are procedurally generated specifically for this repository.
- Muzzle flash, smoke, bullet impact, tracer, crosshair and hit-marker SVG assets are authored specifically for this repository.
